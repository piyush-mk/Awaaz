# Demo script

About three minutes, following Ramesh's day. Run `scripts/smoke.py` once beforehand; it proves every step below.

## Setup (before going on stage)

1. Press **Reset demo data** on `/status`, and check the checklist is green.
2. Laptop on the projector: `/soundbox`. Click once so sound is enabled.
3. Phone: `/app`, mic and camera allowed. Keep `/wa` open on a second screen.
4. With real keys: each demo phone sends "hi" to the WhatsApp test number first.

## Props

- A printed distributor bill (Surf Excel at Rs45 with "Buy 10 Get 1 Free" and no free packet counted).
- A handwritten parchi (Hindi list) and a page from a khata notebook.
- Cash that totals Rs6,700.
- Real products: Maggi, Surf Excel, Colgate, a sugar packet.

## Flow

| Step | Do | Expect |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Morning brief (Salaah) | Play the brief | "Kal Rs8,420 ki sale hui ... ghee sirf do din ka bacha hai" |
| 2. Distributor bill (Godown, Bill scan) | Photograph the bill | Surf Rs3 costlier and missing free packet flagged; confirm updates stock |
| 3. The counter | "Do Maggi, ek Surf Excel, chaar Colgate" | Cart Rs249, Soundbox shows it live |
| 4. Loose item | "Bees rupaye ki cheeni" | "450 gram tolo" |
| 5. Pay | Bill banao, pay | Exact-amount QR, "Rs249 received", stock drops, WhatsApp text and voice receipt |
| 6. Udhaar (Khata) | "Verma ji ke khate mein 500" | Confirm card, then his WhatsApp "Rs500 aapke khate mein jude. Kul baaki Rs1,350"; Postpaid offer (simulated) |
| 7. Customer order | Voice note from the customer on `/wa` | Draft order and packing list with a pay link |
| 8. Closing (Hisaab, Galla) | Photograph the cash | "Rs6,850 hona chahiye tha, Rs6,700 hai. Rs150 kam." |
| 9. Reorder (Salaah) | Draft and send | Purchase order to the supplier on WhatsApp |

## Notes

- The bill-scan sample restocks Colgate. To show "Colgate 8 left", sell the Colgate before scanning the bill.
- Without keys, type the utterances and expect built-in sample results for photos. With keys, check `/status` shows providers as live.
